# Data sourcing options for unresolved QAT requirements

**22 October 2026; decision paper only.** The row IDs refer to [the inventory](DATA_REQUIREMENTS.md) at `recovery/phase-3-recommend-mode` `c8e93aa`. This paper covers requirements with no operational source, a missing implementation, or a source/role outside [I-6](INTENT.md). It does not propose an automatic fallback. Prices are published **indications**, not an account-specific quotation; fees, professional classification, entitlements, internal storage and display/redistribution permissions require direct confirmation before use. “None” in the I-6 column means an IBKR or QAT-internal role already allowed by I-6; it does **not** mean the implementation or licence is approved. DS's 22 October I-6 decision approves operator-supplied ASX calendar/closures, index membership, halts and corporate actions as reference input roles, and sector classification when Phase 4 limits are active; **each file still needs cited provenance and DS approval before use**. Other new decision-bearing source/roles, including new roles for FRED or Yahoo, require a separate DS intent decision. Ranked numbers are **the agent's comparative view**, not DS decisions.

The [10 October real Gateway probe](PHASE3_REAL_GATEWAY_PROBE_2026-10-10.md#authority-file-candidates) is the starting evidence for calendar, membership, halt and action authorities. Its real files were not supplied, and its IBKR bar/quote observations are bounded paper evidence, not a data entitlement audit. The separate shadow build and Thursday probe have priority; neither changes this document's baseline. No purchase or new feed is authorised here.

## Decision boundaries

1. **No present source satisfies the frozen operational authority set.** A public page or paper probe is evidence of possible supply, not a licensed, versioned, complete file. [R05–R09](DATA_REQUIREMENTS.md) stay missing until coverage, provenance and rights are verified.
2. **IBKR-first is conditional, not a declaration of completeness.** Its historical bars/quotes and broker book have probe support; dated ASX 200 membership, three-year halt spans, all corporate actions, a GICS-equivalent sector map, FRED-equivalent macro and ASX news access have not been established through IBKR.
3. **DS decisions remain open:** any ASX/S&P/FRED/Yahoo decision-bearing indirect role or change of existing source; data purchase/licence; whether a legacy factor is removed or given card context/overlay/signal role; Phase 4 limit values/activation; and eventual card transmission. Until then, missing inputs abstain/refuse/alert as stated in the inventory.
