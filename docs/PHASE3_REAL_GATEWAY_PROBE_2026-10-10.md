## Paper broker capability

[IBKR describes a limit-on-open buy](https://www.interactivebrokers.com.au/en/trading/ordertypes.php?m=bracketModal) as `orderType=LMT`, `tif=OPG`, executing at the open at or below the limit and cancelling if unfilled. No other IBKR form found in the reviewed ASX documentation both has an explicit buy limit **and** expires after that opening auction: [IBKR's AUC auction order](https://ibkrcampus.com/campus/glossary-terms/at-auction-order/) may be resubmitted as an ordinary limit after the open. The exact normal order sequence, using QAT's existing broker adapter, is: verify one DU account and no BHP position or working order; obtain the BHP quote; submit one paper BUY **MKT** for that parcel after 10:05 Sydney; verify the holding; submit a paper SELL **STP GTC** for the actual whole-share holding at the tick-rounded 90%-of-reference stop; verify it is working; modify that same stop **up one ASX tick** and verify; cancel it and verify it is absent; submit one paper SELL **MKT** for the actual whole-share holding; verify flat and no working BHP order. No opening-auction order is part of this sequence.
