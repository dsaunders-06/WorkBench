# Ten-symbol reference-authority maintenance — draft for DS decision

Scope: SUN, MFG, CBA, BHP, CSL, WES, WOW, TLS, WDS and TCL. This document proposes a daily check; it does not approve an authority, schedule a job, or wire a file into QAT. QAT must abstain if any of its four authorities is not valid through the card session.

## Proposed daily sequence

After the ASX close and before the 17:30 AEDT shadow-card gate, a named operator checks the [ASX trading calendar and market notices](https://www.asx.com.au/markets/market-resources/trading-hours-calendar/cash-market-trading-hours/trading-calendar) for closures, the [ASX historical announcement service](https://www.asx.com.au/markets/trade-our-cash-market/announcements) for all ten companies' halt, lift, split, consolidation and capital-return notices, and same-dated [STW](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/apac/holdings-daily-au-en-stw.xlsx) and [IOZ](https://www.blackrock.com/au/products/251852/ishares-core-s-p-asx-200-etf/1478358644060.ajax?dataType=fund&fileName=IOZ_holdings&fileType=csv) holdings for membership. Search announcement *types* as well as headlines, distinguish the ten ordinary equities from hybrid notes, and check IBKR daily TRADES bars for zero-volume placeholders and non-integer split-adjusted volume runs. Yahoo split history is a logged cross-check only. Every event admitted to an authority needs its dated primary citation and DS approval.

The operator records the query time, source files and SHA-256 hashes, every new event or explicit absence, discrepancies, and the session reviewed. The first final IBKR bar remains frozen; a later OHLC revision or volume revision above 0.1% blocks that symbol until DS uses the reviewed revision path. No authority validity date advances merely because a scheduled fetch succeeded. If a source is unavailable, a notice is doubtful, or a cross-check reveals an unexplained event, leave the relevant file stale and let all cards abstain.

Only after the full-session review is complete should a proposed file set be produced with `valid_through` equal to that session. DS reviews the changes and hashes, then approves use. Publish the four files as one set outside Git, verify each with its operational loader and confirm their hashes before the 17:30 gate. The calendar's historical coverage through 2027 is separate from its daily validity. Membership entries retain their observed effective dates, with new dated issuer evidence attached as citations.

**Halt-ledger advancement needs a further implementation decision.** Its `valid_through` value is bound into the append-only header hash. Rewriting that value would invalidate accepted entries, and QAT correctly refuses the result. An append-only validity attestation or an explicitly reviewed migration must be designed and tested before daily advancement is enabled. Until then, the 23 October draft expires after that session; do not bypass the check or silently regenerate the chain. This draft leaves the choice to DS.

## IBKR halted flag

IBKR's TWS API [Halted tick type 49](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/tick_types.html) reports 1 for a general halt, 2 for a volatility halt, 0 for not halted, and -1 when status is unavailable. IBKR also says 0 is returned only if the contract is in a TWS watchlist and that not every tick is available for every instrument at all times. [Streaming market data](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/md/streaming-data) requires the relevant market-data access. Thus a positive tick could trigger immediate investigation and an abstention, but missing, -1 or 0 cannot certify that no halt occurred, and a transient tick cannot supply historical completeness. Keep ASX announcements as the authority. Before relying on the flag even as an alert, test its delivery for the ten ASX contracts, paper Gateway subscriptions and a documented halt; make no order request as part of that test.

Dividends are deferred until QAT holds a position. Paper-rehearsal readiness must include an independent missing-dividend detection method, ex-date and cash reconciliation, and DS approval before treating a held position's corporate-action history as complete.
