# Operational authority schema, 22 October 2026

The Phase 3 shadow path freezes the accepted final IBKR bar for each symbol and session. Later observations with different trading values are appended as rejected versions and logged in the operational audit. An OHLC difference or a raw-volume difference **greater than 0.1%** of the frozen volume blocks new shadow cards for that symbol pending DS review. A difference at or below 0.1% is still logged. The record remains outside Git in the OPERATIONAL store.

`scripts/review_shadow_bar_revision.py` previews one symbol/session review without writing by default. Its arguments identify the exact latest material `--expected-revision-hash`, choose `--decision accept_revised` or `--decision keep_original`, and require `--operator DS` and a nonempty `--reason`. Only `--apply` appends the review. Accepting appends a new accepted bar version; keeping leaves the current accepted version untouched. The audit records the operator, reason, decision, observed revision, prior accepted hash, result hash and review time in one transaction. The review clears only that symbol/session block. Any later material revision blocks it again. The script does not connect to a broker or place orders; use it only after DS has inspected the exact bar difference and authorized the decision.

The authority loader accepts HTTPS citations only from `www.asx.com.au`, `asxonline.com`, `www.asxonline.com`, and `announcements.asx.com.au`. ASX identifies ASXOnline as its announcement frontend in its [ComNews Direct guide](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/documents/unlinked-docs/asx-comnews-direct.pdf); the [ASX market-announcement service](https://www.asx.com.au/asx/v2/statistics/announcements.do) serves the announcement PDFs on the ASX domain. The exact-host check rejects lookalike hosts, credentials in URLs, and non-HTTPS links. This whitelist does not imply that an individual notice has been validated; each authority entry still needs its own cited evidence and DS approval before operational use.

Corporate-action authority v2 adds `capital_returns`: each item has a symbol, ex-session, positive exact-decimal `amount_per_share`, and ASX citation. A capital return is **recorded only**. The split factor, analytical prices, share volumes, and dividend field do not use its amount. Existing v1 split and dividend records remain readable. A v2 authority can record a capital return and a consolidation for the same symbol and ex-session separately.
