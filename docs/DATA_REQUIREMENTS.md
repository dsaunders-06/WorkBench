# QAT data requirements and source status

**Inventory date:** 22 October 2026. **Code baseline:** `recovery/phase-3-recommend-mode` at `c8e93aa`. This is a requirements inventory, not permission to connect a new feed, buy data, change an input's role or trade. The separate `build/card-path-shadow` branch is not merged into this baseline; its read-only IBKR client and synthetic-fallback changes are therefore not counted as present here.

The target spans shadow cards, DS-approved cards, QAT-transmitted entries and managed exits, a paper rehearsal, proposed Phase 4 live limits, and the AI advisory screen. The last two are not active trading capabilities. “Exists and validated” means the named behaviour has direct offline tests or the cited paper probe, **not** that a live account, data licence or end-to-end card loop has been validated. “Configured but unvalidated” includes code and settings whose real coverage, authority, finality or operational wiring remains unproved. “Missing” means the required operational input or path is absent. `IBKR` is the I-6 primary authority; `approved indirect` applies only to Yahoo earnings dates used reduce-only. Any other decision-bearing Yahoo/FRED role is `non-compliant` until DS decides it. A local model and QAT's own ledgers are not external market-data sources.

Abbreviations: **S** = shadow, **C** = DS-approved card, **E** = transmitted entry, **X** = managed exit, **P** = paper rehearsal, **L** = proposed Phase 4 live limit, **A** = AI advisory. “Final” means usable for that decision, not merely returned by an API. Evidence links refer to this baseline unless labelled as a probe. No account identifiers, holdings, orders or credentials are reproduced here.

## Unsatisfied inputs and I-6 exceptions

No current operational source satisfies **R05–R09**; R10 lacks an authoritative, complete sector map; R11 lacks the proposed live-limit calculation; R23's app shadow lifecycle is absent. R01–R03, R12 and R22 have source or implementation pieces but do not meet their target operational proof. R04 and R16–R21 have bounded IBKR/paper evidence, with the stated limits; they are not proof of the whole card-to-exit loop. R24–R25 are likewise incomplete end to end.

The sources currently able to affect a recommendation or exposure beyond I-6 are **Yahoo chart/history and latest-trade data, Yahoo fundamentals/relative strength, Yahoo news, and FRED macro**. Synthetic market data, mock fundamentals and mock macro are also selectable/defaulted in this baseline and are not approved trading inputs. Yahoo earnings is the sole approved indirect data role, conditional on reduce-only sizing; that condition is not met by this baseline's risk-ordering example. The Stage A branch addresses some wiring and fallback defects but is not part of this inventory's head. No source change or expanded indirect role is approved by this document.

The [sourcing options](DATA_SOURCING_OPTIONS.md) are limited to these missing/non-compliant rows and distinguish an I-6-compliant broker input from an external authority that would need DS's explicit decision.
